About Eden Rose

Eden Rose is dedicated to capturing the beauty, joy, and romance of your special day. With a keen eye for detail and a passion for storytelling, we blend fine art with photojournalism to create breathtaking images that will be cherished for a lifetime.

Brand Identity

Eden Rose evokes elegance, romance, and timeless beauty. With a refined color palette of soft hues, whites, and blacks, and sophisticated typography, the brand reflects professionalism, creativity, and a deep understanding of the love and emotions that come with saying “I do.”

Typography

The main font, The Seasons Light, exudes elegance and sophistication, perfectly capturing the essence of Eden Rose. Paired with Montserrat, this combination enhances readability and balance.
